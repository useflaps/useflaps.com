PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: 2026-10-01

01. OVERVIEW

FLAPS is designed to deliver a focused, reliable productivity experience. We collect and process minimal data strictly necessary to operate, maintain, debug, and improve application functionality.

02. DATA WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on user settings and platform permissions, we may collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable diagnostic, technical, and usage telemetry, including but not limited to:

  • Crash reports, stack traces, and system error logs.
  • Aggregated performance metrics and launch duration.
  • Feature engagement and flight session completion statistics.
  • Device architecture, operating system version, and locale settings.

We do not collect or store sensitive personal information, identity credentials, or location tracking coordinates.

03. USE OF INFORMATION

Collected diagnostic information is processed solely for performance analysis, bug fixing, infrastructure monitoring, and user experience enhancements.

04. THIRD-PARTY INFRASTRUCTURE

We may process operational telemetry through vetted infrastructure partners (including but not limited to Apple App Store Analytics, TestFlight, or privacy-compliant diagnostic services). We do not sell, rent, or trade usage data to advertising networks or external data brokers.

05. USER CHOICE & OPT-IN

Where applicable, telemetry and diagnostics may be toggled or disabled via system-level iOS privacy controls or directly within app preferences.

06. INQUIRIES

Direct all privacy and data inquiries to privacy@useflaps.com.
